## 2. Commercial licence

A commercial licence removes the AGPL's copyleft obligations and is what you
want if any of these apply:

- You want to offer ChatBranch, or a modified version, **as a hosted service**
  to your own customers without publishing your modifications.
- You want to **embed** it in a closed-source product you distribute.
- Your organisation's policy forbids AGPL dependencies.
- You want the **commercial-only modules** (see *Editions* in the
  [README](README.md#editions)) — AI voice calls and voice cloning, token
  credits and billing, multi-outlet / teams / roles, and marketplace buyer

  AGPL grant above.

A commercial licence also carries a support commitment and the managed-hosting
option.
